# JQC/PWI Management — Technician Quick Guide

One page for everyday use. Open **JQC/PWI Management** on your computer, tablet or phone.

## Find what you need to do
1. Tap **My Training** (on a phone it's in the bar at the bottom).
2. The **Pending** tab lists everything waiting for you. Overdue items are at the top in red.
3. Items marked **New Revision – Please Re-Sign** mean a work instruction changed. Read *What changed* and sign off again.

Nothing pending? You'll see "Nothing pending — you're all caught up."

## Sign off a PWI (work instruction)
1. In **My Training**, tap the PWI.
2. Tap **Open document** and read it. Your trainer's name is shown if you need help.
3. Under **Training log**, tap **Choose a file** and pick your training log (PDF, Word, Excel, JPG or PNG, up to 25 MB).
4. Tap **Upload Training Log** and wait for "Training log uploaded".
5. Tap **Sign Off**. The item moves to the **Signed Off** tab.

**Sign Off** only turns on after the upload. If you see *"This revision is no longer current"*, the document has just been revised: go back to My Training and open the new item.

## JQCs (job qualification checklists)
You don't sign off JQCs yourself. Your manager does.

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Needs Training** | Your supervisor will put you into training |
| **In Training – Waiting on Supervisor** | Train with your trainer. Your supervisor will sign off the checklist when you're qualified |
| **Signed Off** | You're qualified on the current revision |

If a JQC is revised, it goes back to **Needs Training** with a **New Revision** badge.

## What the colours mean
Status is always written out as well as coloured.

- 🟢 **Signed Off**: done, on the current revision
- 🟠 **Pending**, **Re-Sign Required**, **Needs Training**, **In Training**: still to do
- 🔴 **Overdue**: past its due date
- ⚪ **Not Assigned**

## Other screens
- **JQCs / PWIs**: look up and open any document.
- **Matrix**: who's qualified on what.
- **Cross-Training**: which tasks need more trained people.
- **Employees**: the team list. Tap your own name to see your history and earlier sign-offs.

## Problems?
- **"Read-only access"** and you should have items: ask your manager to check your email in the employee list.
- **An upload fails**: check the file type and size, then try again. If it still fails, send your manager the message shown.
